Crimson Player is a minimalist open-source music player, currently developed for Android.

Features in the preview:
• Play local audio files using the Media Kit engine.
• Browse songs and albums found on your device.
• Search local music, manage a playback queue and mark favorites.
• Light, dark and automatic appearance.
• Animated bottom navigation and a mini-player.
• Open directly accessible HTTPS audio streams you have the right to use (experimental).

The app does not provide a built-in Spotify or YouTube Music streaming catalog, nor does it download protected songs.

Crimson Player is an early preview. Some functionality still requires on-device testing. Playlists, background controls, desktop support and online catalogs are planned.
